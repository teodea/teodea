### 🏟️ Fantacarriera — the one I'd want you to look at

A career-mode fantasy football manager for Serie A. Clubs carry multi-year contracts, a
wage bill, three ledgers, a stadium and a youth squad, so a season has consequences instead
of a reset. Eight people play in it every week; a 41-chapter written constitution is the
spec, and the software is its enforcement.

Next.js 16 + FastAPI + Firestore, both services on Cloud Run. Built solo in six months:
1,193 commits, ~192k lines, 347 test files, 68 architecture decisions written down before
the code.

**→ [Walk through the live app](https://fantacarriera.app)** — click *Esplora la Lega*: no
signup, real data, controls switched off.
**→ [What it is and how it's built](https://github.com/teodea/fantacarriera)**

*(Source is private — a real league plays in there. Happy to walk through any of it.)*
