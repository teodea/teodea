## 🏟️ Fantacarriera

The one I care about most. It's a fantasy football manager where a club is a real going concern: multi year contracts, a wage bill you have to cover, a stadium, a youth squad, and a trophy cabinet that remembers. Sell a striker in October and you'll still feel it two seasons later.

The rules live in a written constitution that the league votes on, and the software is what enforces it. Turning a document eight humans argue about into code that can't be argued with is genuinely my favourite part.

## 🧰 Other things I've made

**[Gross to net salary calculator](https://teodea.github.io/jethr-task/)**
Italian payslips are a black box. This one opens it: every single line of the tax waterfall, each with a plain language explanation and the actual law it comes from. Works for all 7.896 municipalities, because your comune changes the answer. No dependencies, it's just a page that opens.

**[Automated soccer vision](https://github.com/teodea/automated-soccer-vision)**
Point it at TV footage of a match and it figures out who's who, which team they're on, and how fast they're actually running in real metres. YOLOv11 for detection, ByteTrack to keep the IDs straight, and a bit of geometry to flatten the pitch into a top down view.

**[arXiv paper classifier](https://github.com/teodea/arxiv-paper-classifier)** and **[traffic network centrality](https://github.com/teodea/traffic-network-centrality)**
Two from my Miami years. One reads a paper's title and abstract and guesses its category, the other asks whether the mathematically important roads in Florida are the ones people actually drive on. (Sometimes yes.)

**[Digital wallet](https://github.com/teodea/digital-wallet)** and **[Snake on a DE1-SoC](https://github.com/teodea/snake-game-de1soc-controlled)**
The other end of the stack, from when the fun was making hardware blink. A wallet protocol written directly on TCP sockets, and Snake in C that you steer by tilting the board.
